Insurance Claims FAQ

Do I really pay $0 out of pocket? +

In most cases, yes. Your homeowner's insurance covers the full cost of storm and impact damage repairs. You may have a deductible depending on your policy, but the repair itself and all documentation services are covered. We'll review your policy details during the assessment.

What if my insurance already made a low offer? +

This is actually our specialty. We provide supplemental documentation and detailed damage reports that justify a higher payout. Many of our clients have had their claims increased by 3-4x after we provide professional documentation to the adjuster.

Is the 72-hour window really that important? +

Yes. Most Florida insurance policies require you to report damage promptly, typically within 72 hours of the event. Waiting longer can give the insurance company grounds to reduce or deny your claim. Even if you're past 72 hours, contact us immediately — we can still help in many cases.

What does the free damage assessment include? +

We send a licensed inspector to your property who will photograph all damage, measure affected areas, identify damage you may have missed, and provide a written assessment. This documentation alone is worth hundreds of dollars but is completely free when you choose us for the repair.

Can you help if my claim was denied? +

Absolutely. Denied claims can often be reopened with proper documentation. We'll assess your damage, provide professional evidence, and help you file a supplement or appeal. Many denied claims are approved once proper documentation is submitted.

Do you work with all insurance companies? +

Yes. We work with all major Florida insurance carriers including Citizens, State Farm, Allstate, Universal, Heritage, and dozens more. Our documentation format is designed to meet every carrier's requirements for claim approval.
